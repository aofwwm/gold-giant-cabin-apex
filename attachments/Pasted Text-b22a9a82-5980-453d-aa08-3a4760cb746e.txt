==================================================
THE CURRENT TRAVEL PRODUCT HAS TWO FUNDAMENTAL PROBLEMS
==================================================

PROBLEM 1:

Kairos currently jumps too quickly from:

preferences
→ places
→ itinerary

without first understanding:

WHAT ARE THE DEFINING EXPERIENCES OF THIS DESTINATION?

A first-time Chengdu itinerary that misses most of:

pandas
Dujiangyan
major heritage
major food experiences
major nature options
important city districts

has failed even if the route between its chosen obscure POIs is efficient.

Likewise:

a first-time Singapore itinerary that misses most of:

Marina Bay
Merlion / waterfront
Gardens by the Bay
Sentosa
major wildlife attractions
heritage districts
major food experiences

has failed.

==================================================

PROBLEM 2:

After the first itinerary is generated, Travel is too static.

The user currently receives:

text itinerary cards
+
a separate map
+
an almost empty Places tab

The user should instead continuously be able to:

discover places
understand why they are worth visiting
see photos
save them
add them
remove them
move them
see where they are
understand which day they fit
understand how to travel between them

Travel planning is an ongoing refinement process.
