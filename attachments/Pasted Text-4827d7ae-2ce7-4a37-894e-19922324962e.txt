Add an important transparency layer to ORACLE.

The user must understand that:

KAIROS DOES NOT USE EVERY DIVINATION METHOD FOR EVERY QUESTION.

The methods selected depend on:

1. what the user is asking
2. what information is available
3. which divination systems are genuinely relevant to that type of question

==================================================
BEFORE THE QUESTION
==================================================

Add concise explanatory copy:

HOW ORACLE WORKS

“Kairos chooses the divination methods that best fit your question. A timing question may use Tong Shu, while a decision may call for Tarot or I Ching.

Different questions, different signs.”

Keep this concise and premium.

Do not make it sound technical.

==================================================
AFTER QUESTION CLASSIFICATION
==================================================

Once Kairos understands the question, show:

KAIROS SELECTED · X METHODS

“Based on what you're asking, these methods are most relevant.”

Then list each selected method AND WHY IT WAS SELECTED.

Example question:

“Should I accept this new job?”

Show:

TAROT
For the opportunities, tensions and emotional dynamics around the decision.

I CHING · 易经
For understanding change, direction and how the situation may develop.

NUMEROLOGY
For adding your current personal timing cycle to the reading.

==================================================
TIMING EXAMPLE
==================================================

Question:

“Which day next week should I launch?”

Potential UI:

KAIROS SELECTED · 3 METHODS

TONG SHU · 通胜
Chosen because you're asking about auspicious timing.

NUMEROLOGY
Chosen to compare the numerical character of the candidate dates.

I CHING · 易经
Chosen for guidance around initiation, change and direction.

==================================================
RELATIONSHIP EXAMPLE
==================================================

Question:

“What is happening with this relationship?”

Potential:

TAROT
Chosen to explore the emotional dynamics and underlying tensions.

CHINESE ZODIAC
Chosen for compatibility and relationship context, if birth information is available.

BAZI · 八字
Chosen for deeper personalised compatibility only if sufficient birth information exists.

Do NOT use Tong Shu unless the question involves timing.

==================================================
METHOD SELECTION EXPLANATION
==================================================

Each selected method should have:

method name
status
one short sentence explaining WHY it was chosen

Example structured data:

{
  "method": "tong_shu",
  "selected": true,
  "reason": "The question asks which date is most suitable for an important action."
}

Another:

{
  "method": "tarot",
  "selected": true,
  "reason": "The question involves uncertainty around a personal decision."
}

==================================================
IMPORTANT
==================================================

The explanation must correspond to the actual method-selection logic.

Do NOT let the frontend invent generic reasons after the fact.

The same classification that selects the method should provide the reason.

==================================================
NOT SELECTED
==================================================

Under:

[ View all methods ]

users may also see why something was not selected.

Example:

TONG SHU
Not used
This question is not primarily about date or timing.

BAZI
Not used
Birth information is not available.

ASTROLOGY
Not connected

Keep this secondary.

Do not clutter the main reading.

==================================================
DEFAULT EXPERIENCE
==================================================

The main flow should feel like:

USER ASKS
↓
KAIROS UNDERSTANDS THE QUESTION
↓
KAIROS SELECTS RELEVANT DIVINATION METHODS
↓
USER SEES WHICH METHODS WERE SELECTED + WHY
↓
THE METHODS GENERATE THEIR ACTUAL SIGNALS
↓
神婆 INTERPRETS THEM

This transparency is a core part of Oracle.

==================================================
COPY RULE
==================================================

Use the term:

DIVINATION

not:

DIVINITY

when referring to Tarot, Tong Shu, I Ching, etc.

==================================================
ACCEPTANCE TEST
==================================================

Question:

“Is Friday a good day to sign the contract?”

Expected:

Kairos explicitly explains why Tong Shu was selected.

Question:

“Should I change jobs?”

Expected:

Kairos may select Tarot / I Ching / Numerology and explains why.

Tong Shu should not automatically be included unless timing is part of the question.

Question:

“Are we compatible?”

Expected:

relationship-relevant methods are selected.

Each selected method includes a concise reason.

The user should always be able to answer:

“Why did Kairos use these divination methods for my question?”
