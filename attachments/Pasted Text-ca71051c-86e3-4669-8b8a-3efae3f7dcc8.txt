==================================================
5. ANCHOR PACK BUILDER
==================================================

Create something conceptually like:

buildDestinationAnchorPack(destination)

It should:

1. resolve canonical destination

2. retrieve legitimate destination knowledge

3. identify destination-defining experience groups

4. identify verified major / iconic places

5. classify places into geographic areas

6. assign importance tier

7. identify typical visit duration where defensible

8. identify day-trip / excursion relationships

9. identify sensible day-pattern combinations

10. validate coordinates / identity

11. store provenance

12. save the resulting pack
