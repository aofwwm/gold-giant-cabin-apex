PROBLEM 6
FOOD / CAFE RECOMMENDATIONS HAVE NO EXPLANATION
==================================================

I am not sure why Kairos chooses particular:

restaurants

cafes

dessert places

The current itinerary needs stronger reasoning.

Food stops absolutely CAN belong in a Travel itinerary.

But they should have a PURPOSE.

==================================================
FOOD STOP PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Do NOT randomly add:

Cafe A

because the day needs another place.

A food stop should solve something.

Examples:

MEAL TIMING

local cuisine

high-quality option near current route

natural rest point

scenic café

special destination restaurant

family-friendly meal

fast meal on a busy sightseeing day

late-night food near hotel

rain-friendly break

iconic local food experience

==================================================
FOOD STOP TYPES
==================================================

Distinguish:

MEAL DESTINATION

Worth travelling specifically for.

Example:
famous local restaurant / signature cuisine.

ROUTE-FIT MEAL

Good restaurant chosen because it lies naturally
between two itinerary stops.

QUICK MEAL

Prioritises convenience and time.

CAFE / REST BREAK

Used because the day's walking/pace benefits from a break.

SCENIC CAFE

The venue itself contributes to the experience.

LOCAL SPECIALTY

Selected to expose traveller to a food closely associated
with that destination.

==================================================
WHY THIS FOOD STOP
==================================================

Every automatically recommended restaurant/café should have a
concise reason.

Example:

LUNCH · LOCAL SPECIALTY

Why here
Known for Kamakura-style shirasu dishes and only
6 min from your next stop.

or:

CAFE BREAK

Why here
Fits your 45-minute gap and avoids a 20-minute detour.

or:

DINNER · NEAR HOTEL

Why here
Good fit after a long sightseeing day and only
8 min back to your hotel.

==================================================
WANDERLOG-STYLE FUNCTIONALITY
==================================================

planners:

- daily itinerary
- restaurants alongside attractions
- map-linked places
- travel time between stops
- place details
- nearby discovery
- category-based recommendations

KAIROS should improve on this by making recommendations CONTEXTUAL.

The question is not merely:

“What restaurants exist?”

It is:

“Which restaurant makes sense here, at this time, for this
traveller, on this route?”
